# HIMPACT Lab: Himalayan Hydroclimatology Impact Research Lab

Website for **HIMPACT Lab**, School of Civil and Environmental Engineering (SCENE), **IIT Mandi**, Himachal Pradesh, India.

We study how climate change affects water resources in the Himalaya: floods, droughts and hydroclimatic extremes. Our methods combine hydrological and atmospheric modelling, remote sensing and deep learning.

**Lead:** [Dr. Vivek Gupta](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=bMLz1QgAAAAJ&hl=en), Assistant Professor, IIT Mandi

## Research themes
- Climate change impact assessment
- Droughts and climate extremes
- Early warning and climate information systems
- Hydrological modelling (numerical and data-driven)
- Coupled atmospheric–hydrological modelling
- AI and deep learning for water resources

## Editing the site
The whole site is one static page, `index.html`, with no build step. Edit it and push to `main`, and GitHub Pages redeploys it in about a minute.

- **Add a publication:** copy one `<li class="pub">` block in the Publications section. Set `data-k` to `journal`, `chapter` or `conf`, then update the counts on the filter buttons and in the stats strip.
- **Add a team member:** copy one `<article class="person">` block in the Team section.
- **Add news:** add an `<li>` at the top of the `#news` timeline.
